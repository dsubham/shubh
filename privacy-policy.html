Privacy Policy

Your privacy and your child's safety are important to us. This policy explains how KEYS – Kognitics Early Years School collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

This Privacy Policy applies to KEYS – Kognitics Early Years School ("KEYS", "we", "us", or "our"), located at 214, Varthur Road, Vinayaka Nagar, Gunjur Post, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560087. By using our website or enrolling your child, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information to provide you with the best possible experience and to facilitate your child's education. The information we may collect includes:

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Parent or guardian name, email address, and phone number
  • Child's name, age, and date of birth
  • Home or residential address
  • Medical information relevant to your child's care and safety
  • Emergency contact details
  • Messages or enquiries submitted through our website forms
  • Admission-related documents and records

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Browser type and version
  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Pages visited on our website and time spent on each page
  • Referring website or search engine
  • Device type (mobile, tablet, desktop)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To process admission enquiries and respond to questions
  • To communicate updates, school events, circulars, and important notices
  • To ensure your child's safety and well-being while in our care
  • To maintain attendance records and academic progress
  • To improve our website and communication materials
  • To send newsletters, programme updates, or promotional content (with your consent)
  • To comply with legal or regulatory requirements applicable to educational institutions

3. Children's Privacy

We take special care regarding the privacy of children. We collect and process children's personal information only as necessary for educational and safety purposes. We do not:

  • Share children's photographs or personal information publicly without explicit parental consent
  • Sell or commercially exploit any information related to children
  • Allow children to directly submit personal information through our website

All photographs or videos featuring children on our website, social media, or promotional materials are used only with the prior written consent of parents or guardians.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Kognitics – The International School: As KEYS is a unit of Kognitics, relevant student and parent data may be shared with the parent institution for administrative and educational purposes.
  • With service providers: Trusted third-party vendors who assist in website hosting, email delivery, or payment processing — bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • As required by law: If compelled by a court order, regulatory body, or applicable law to disclose information.
  • For health and safety emergencies: To medical professionals or emergency services if your child's safety is at risk.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include:

  • Secure HTTPS connections on our website
  • Access controls limiting who can view personal records
  • Regular review of our data handling practices

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website may use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to improve your browsing experience. Cookies help us understand how visitors use our website and allow us to remember your preferences.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of the website.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites such as Google Maps or social media platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external websites and encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.

8. Retention of Data

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Student records are typically retained for a minimum of five years following the completion of enrolment, after which they are securely deleted or anonymised.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you or your child
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to legal obligations
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info_gunjur@keysedu.in.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Any significant changes will be communicated via our website or by email. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
